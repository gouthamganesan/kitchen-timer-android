# Evidence summary: 2026-11-17

This public summary preserves results without publishing raw device logs, personal paths or identifiers. The detailed scope is in the [test matrix](TEST-MATRIX.md).

The local suite contains 31 tests: 9 core, 13 haptic policy and 9 Android platform tests. Version 0.1.2 compiles and passes the suite with zero lint errors. Emulator flows established controls, themes, picker commit/cancel, expanded notification actions, a real background expiry and process-death recovery. System events independently confirmed the process restart.

Visual review found defects beyond action assertions: an inaccessible compact picker footer, a partly clipped landscape sound switch and dial movement when the LCD acquired an hours field. These were repaired and rechecked. Two measured full windings then reached exactly 2:00:00. Earlier failed local logs were retained rather than relabeled as successful.

The final 0.1.2 refinement removes the rectangular ripple around the sound/vibration row. Emulator toggling and fresh [screenshots](../images) cover both positions and all four appearances. The updated APK was installed on the connected phone and its version verified. No physical-feel, DND, lock/Doze or full accessibility result is inferred from that installation.

Raw builds, APKs, screenshots and diagnostic evidence remain under ignored local `artifacts/`. Source snapshots, public imagery and this result summary are included in Git; generated logs and device records are not.
