Time, with a little weight.

Three kitchen timer studies. Turn a dial. Feel out a direction.

Try the layers

Cream mechanical

Moulded plastic

The familiar kitchen drawer timer. A heavy cream dial, a raised grip, and a red index mark. The simplest, most literal direction.Fixed scale · rotating grip · layered red indicator

Enamel & chrome

Polished metal

A little countertop jewel. Warm pink enamel, a reflective metal bezel, and an orange disc beneath glass. The most expressive direction.Layered chrome · domed cap · glazed dial

The red disc

Rubber & paper

Time you can see disappearing. A charcoal bumper around a warm white face. Full hours layer up in red; extra minutes deepen the shade.Soft square housing · disappearing disc · small turn knob

Drag clockwise to add time. Each full turn adds an hour and leaves a coloured layer. The next partial turn is darker. Tap the readout for exact hours and minutes. Arrow keys adjust by a minute; Shift adjusts by five.

About this prototype

All three are drawn in HTML, CSS and SVG. No flattened timer images. Start, pause, reset and the sound/vibrate switch work while this page is open. Browser vibration requires a supported device. Android background alarms are outside this visual prototype.
